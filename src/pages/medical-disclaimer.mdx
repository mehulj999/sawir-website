South Asian Women in Rare shares personal stories, experiences, and conversations about living with or supporting someone with a rare disease. This content is for **informational and community purposes only**.

## This is not medical advice

Nothing on this site — podcast episodes, blog posts, guest stories, or any other content — is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is not a substitute for professional care.

- We are not doctors, and most guests are not speaking as medical professionals.
- Every rare disease and every person's experience is different — what worked for one guest may not be appropriate for you.
- Never delay seeking medical advice, disregard professional guidance, or start/stop any treatment because of something you heard or read here.

## In an emergency

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately, or go to your nearest emergency room.

## Talk to a professional

For any health concern, please consult a qualified healthcare provider familiar with your situation and history.
